Case ID:

Created:

Employee Question:
my manager is sending me rude messages


Policy Section:
2.3 Harassment

Policy Page:
16

Assessment:
Based on the Code of Business Ethics, if your team manager treats you with disrespect, this could be classified as harassment (Source 1, Page 16). Rude messages from your manager could be considered a form of disrespectful treatment.

The Code states that "We all have a right to be treated with respect and courtesy, and a duty to treat others this way" and that the Group maintains a work environment free of harassment (Source 5, Page 16).

If you are facing an issue involving harassment, you are encouraged to discuss it openly. You can raise the issue directly with your team manager. If you feel uncomfortable with this approach, you should discuss the matter with your human resources manager instead (Source 1, Page 16).
